\chapter{Applications}\label{ch:sec03}

Building on the quantum primitives of \cref{ch:sec02}, this part turns to
applications. The main application areas are:
\begin{itemize}
  \item ground-state energy estimation;
  \item amplitude estimation;
  \item the HHL algorithm for linear systems;
  \item solving Poisson's equation and the heat equation.
\end{itemize}
The remaining chapters develop the tools needed for the last of these ---
simulating partial differential equations on a quantum computer --- beginning
with Hamiltonian simulation (\cref{ch:sec04}).
