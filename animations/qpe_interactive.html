Quantum phase estimation — interactive walkthrough

Standard QPE with t = 3 counting qubits (grid size T = 2t = 8) on a unitary with eigenphase φ, U|ψ⟩ = e2πiφ|ψ⟩. The three stages (Example 4.21): (1) Hadamards build the uniform superposition; (2) the controlled powers kick back the phase e2πiφj onto ancilla |j⟩; (3) the inverse QFT concentrates the comb and we measure k. The outcome amplitude is exactly the γk of Theorem 4.24, and the bars below are |γk|². Dyadic φ = k/T give a single deterministic outcome; a non-dyadic φ spreads over a peaked comb.

Theorem 4.24 — the amplitude γk and the precision bound

γk = (1/T) ∑j=0T−1 e2πi(φ−k/T)j = (1/T) · (1 − e2πiT(φ−k/T)) / (1 − e2πi(φ−k/T))   (= 1 when φ = k/T).
Tail bound:  P(|φ − k̃/T|1 ≥ ε) ≤ 1/(2Tε) + 1/(2(Tε)²)   — here |·|1 is wrap-around (circular) distance.
